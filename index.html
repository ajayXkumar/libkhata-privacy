Privacy Policy

Lumina · Effective 2 May 2026 · Last updated 2 May 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Lumina mobile application (“Lumina”, “the app”, “we”) collects, uses, and protects your data. The app is operated by Ajay Kumar Saharan (contact: ajaymatrix386@gmail.com).

1. Information we collect

We collect only the data needed to operate the app. Categories:

  • Account identifiers — Google account email, display name, and Firebase user ID, captured when you sign in.
  • Profile information — Name and phone number that you (or your library admin) enter while registering. For library members, an optional Aadhaar number can be stored by the library admin for verification. Only the last four digits are visible inside the student app.
  • Activity data — Study session start/end times, durations, focus session notes you enter, attendance check-in/check-out times, daily goals, streak data, and aggregate stats used to power achievements and themes.
  • Library context (library members only) — Library ID, seat assignment, plan, payment records, and any payment-receipt photo you (or your library admin) upload.
  • Device data — Crash logs and diagnostic data collected by Firebase Crashlytics if a crash occurs. This includes device model, OS version, and a stack trace, attached to your Firebase user ID.

We do not collect contacts, location, browsing history, photos outside of payment receipts you choose to upload, microphone audio, or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use the information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To compute your study stats, attendance, streaks, achievements, and progress visualisations.
  • To let your library administrator manage seats, plans, payments, and broadcasts (library members only).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any third-party marketing.

3. Where data is stored

All server-side data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Storage, Firebase Crashlytics). Firebase is operated by Google LLC. Some data may also be cached locally on your device (AsyncStorage) so the app works offline.

Transit between the app and Firebase is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing. Limited sharing happens only:

  • With your library administrator, who can see profile and activity data for students enrolled in their library. Standalone (no-library) users are not visible to any admin.
  • With Google Firebase as our infrastructure provider.
  • If required by law, valid legal process, or to protect against fraud or abuse.

5. Data retention & deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing ajaymatrix386@gmail.com from the Google account linked to your profile. Within 30 days we will:

  • Delete your student/admin profile document.
  • Delete your study sessions, attendance records, goals, payments, achievements, and theme preferences.
  • Delete your Firebase Authentication record.

Crash diagnostic data older than 90 days is automatically purged by Firebase Crashlytics.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not use Lumina. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Security

We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure students can only access their own records, library admins can only access records inside their own library, and unauthenticated requests are rejected. Sign-in uses Google Identity Services with no password stored on our side.

8. Your rights

  • Access — You can view your data inside the app at any time.
  • Correction — Profile fields can be edited from your profile screen.
  • Deletion — Email us as described in section 5.
  • Portability — Email us and we will export your data as JSON.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the “Last updated” date above and notify users in-app where appropriate. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: ajaymatrix386@gmail.com
